ABOUT THE GUPTA'S LANDSCAPE

Transforming Outdoor Spaces Through Design, Nature & Craftsmanship

The Gupta’s Landscape is a Kolkata-based professional landscaping and outdoor development company with 5 years of experience in creating distinctive, functional, and visually refined outdoor environments.

Our work brings together landscape design, natural elements, quality materials, skilled execution, and thoughtful detailing to transform open spaces into environments that enhance the character and usability of a property.

From residential gardens and commercial landscapes to institutional and hospitality spaces, we provide comprehensive landscaping solutions tailored to the unique requirements of every site.

Our Approach

Every successful landscape begins with understanding the space.

"We consider the site, surroundings, intended use, budget, material requirements, maintenance needs, and client’s expectations before planning the execution. Our approach focuses on quality, functionality, attention to detail, professional supervision, and dependable project coordination."

We believe landscaping is more than simply adding plants or decorative elements. It is about creating a balanced environment where nature, architecture, design, and functionality work together.
